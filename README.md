Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Roast Your Own Agent"**
(`u-mfnakmal-roast-your-own-agent-2d221bda`).

`main.py` writes one original one-line roast of my own AI agent to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> I asked my agent for one roast — it wrote a plan, a skill, and a cron job to remind me to laugh later.

One line, 102 characters, playful and self-deprecating — it roasts the
agent's habit of turning every tiny task into a plan, a skill, and a
scheduled job, instead of just doing the thing.
